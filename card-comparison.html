Key difference

  • Glass → Aurora grid bleeds through. Cards feel lighter, more layered. Closer to a frosted panel.
  • Solid → Cards are opaque, higher contrast against the background. Aurora is fully visible in the gaps between cards, not behind them. Crisper "command center" feel.
  • Hover behavior is identical in both versions.
