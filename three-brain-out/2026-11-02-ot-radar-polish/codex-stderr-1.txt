Other transaction authority, explained

Other transactions (OTs) and commercial solutions openings (CSOs) are faster, more flexible ways for agencies to buy. Here’s how they work, and which ones are open on SAM.gov right now. A person reads every notice before it goes on the list.

What is an OT?

A flexible agreement that isn’t a standard contract, grant or cooperative agreement, so most FAR rules don’t apply and the terms are negotiated. DoD prototype OTs use 10 U.S.C. 4022.

What is a CSO?

A commercial solutions opening is a competition built for commercial products and services, under 10 U.S.C. 3458. Awards are fixed-price contracts or, in some cases, OTs.

Do I need a consortium?

Sometimes. Many OTs run through a consortium, and those usually take submissions from members. Others are open to any company. Each notice says which kind it is.
