Other transaction authority, explained

Other transactions (OTs) and commercial solutions openings (CSOs) give agencies different ways to buy. Here’s a plain-English introduction, followed by public SAM.gov notices we’ve read.

Get new OT and CSO notices in a free Monday email
What is an OT?

An OT is a flexible agreement outside the usual contract, grant or cooperative agreement process. The terms are negotiated, and most rules in the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) don’t apply.

What is a CSO?

A commercial solutions opening invites proposals for commercial products and services. Awards can be fixed-price contracts or, in some cases, OTs.

Do I need a consortium?

It depends on the notice. Some opportunities go through a consortium and may require membership. Others accept submissions directly from companies.
