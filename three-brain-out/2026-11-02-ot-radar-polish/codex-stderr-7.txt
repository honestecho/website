Other transaction authority, explained

Other transactions (OTs) and commercial solutions openings (CSOs) give agencies different ways to buy products and services. Start with the basics below, then browse public SAM.gov notices we’ve read.

Get the free Monday email
What is an OT?

An OT is a flexible agreement outside the usual contract, grant or cooperative agreement process. The terms are negotiated, and most rules in the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) don’t apply.

What is a CSO?

A commercial solutions opening invites proposals for commercial products and services. Awards can be fixed-price contracts or, in some cases, OTs.

Do I need a consortium?

For some opportunities, you’ll submit through a consortium and may need to become a member. For others, you can submit directly.
